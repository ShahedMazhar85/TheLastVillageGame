The Last Village

Enemies never stop coming. Hold out for 10 minutes.

  • Shoot enemies — WASD to move, mouse to aim, click to fire
  • Collect gold — walk near coins they drop
  • Click a tower spot to build a tower
  • Click a tower to upgrade it
  • Click the town core to repair it
  • Click the forge to upgrade your weapon
If the town core falls, you lose. Press M to mute.
